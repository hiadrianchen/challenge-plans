# Kyoto trip plan

A 4-day Kyoto trip in mid-July for two people.

Day 1: Land at 14:00, drop bags, walk Fushimi Inari, then Gion for dinner.
Day 2: Arashiyama bamboo grove, monkey park, boat ride, Kinkaku-ji, Nijo Castle, then Pontocho at night.
Day 3: Day trip to Nara — deer park, Todai-ji, Kasuga shrine — back for a kaiseki dinner at a famous place.
Day 4: Last-minute shopping, then catch the 10:00 shinkansen to the airport.

Budget: book the non-refundable flights now to lock the low fare. We'll sort hotels later.
